# Product Definitions

The source section carries no prose of its own beyond this heading: the
instructions move straight into the 13 data tables, each of which is a file
here. In the order the instructions present them:

**Inflows**

- [`I.A` — Inflows-Assets](I.A-inflows-assets.md)
- [`I.S` — Inflows-Secured](I.S-inflows-secured.md)
- [`I.U` — Inflows-Unsecured](I.U-inflows-unsecured.md)
- [`I.O` — Inflows-Other](I.O-inflows-other.md)

**Outflows**

- [`O.D` — Outflows-Deposits](O.D-outflows-deposits.md)
- [`O.S` — Outflows-Secured](O.S-outflows-secured.md)
- [`O.W` — Outflows-Wholesale](O.W-outflows-wholesale.md)
- [`O.O` — Outflows-Other](O.O-outflows-other.md)

**Supplemental**

- [`S.B` — Balance Sheet](S.B-supplemental-balance-sheet.md)
- [`S.DC` — Derivatives & Collateral](S.DC-supplemental-derivatives-collateral.md)
- [`S.FX` — Foreign Exchange](S.FX-supplemental-foreign-exchange.md)
- [`S.I` — Informational](S.I-supplemental-informational.md)
- [`S.L` — Liquidity Risk Measurement (LRM)](S.L-supplemental-lrm.md)

Each table numbers its products with a PID, so a product is cited throughout
the instructions as table plus PID — `O.D.1` for Transactional Accounts,
`I.S.4` for Collateral Swaps. The appendices and the mapping documents in
Appendices VI, VII and VIII use those same identifiers.
